    Data dictionary & caveats

    This dashboard uses the supplied research extract. It does not automatically fetch or update bank filings. Source links are entry points; each bank-year metric must ultimately be tied to a specific annual-report page, table or RBI dataset row.

    • Profit: net profit attributable to the bank, ₹ crore.
    • CRAR: capital to risk-weighted assets ratio, percent.
    • Total business: deposits plus gross advances; confirm domestic/global scope before cross-bank comparison.
    • Market capitalisation: listed equity market value, ₹ crore, same valuation date required.
    • Five-year trend data: figures and ratios as supplied in the research exchange; some profiles have merger or reconciliation caveats.
    • No overall Top 50 ranking is displayed until the complete bank universe passes data-quality checks.
