How the pilot score is calculated

  1. Rank each of the 12 banks separately on FY26 net profit, FY26 CRAR and FY26 net-profit growth over FY25.
  2. Convert each rank into a percentile from 0 to 100. The highest observation receives 100 and the lowest receives 0; ties receive average ranks.
  3. Multiply the three percentile scores by 40%, 30% and 30%, respectively.
  4. Add the weighted contributions. The result is a relative score out of 100 for the PSB peer group only.
Source for FY25/FY26 net profit and CRAR: Government of India, Department of Financial Services, Banking Analytics. This pilot does not include data from third-party NPA compilations.

Scoring and comparability rules

    Use peer-group percentile normalisation; merger discontinuities must be adjusted; missing values must not be treated as zero. The PSB pilot is limited to three metrics; full composite scores remain withheld until minimum completeness thresholds are met.

    Source register

    Official repositories and annual-report entry points.

    Data dictionary, downloads & caveats

    • Profit: net profit, ₹ crore; confirm standalone basis and exceptional items.
    • CRAR: capital to risk-weighted assets ratio, percent.
    • Total business: deposits plus gross advances; confirm domestic/global scope.
    • Market capitalisation: listed equity market value, ₹ crore, same valuation date required.
    • Merger discontinuities, accounting restatements and reporting scope must be reconciled before CAGR calculation.
    • No overall Top 50 ranking is displayed until the eligible universe passes data-quality checks.

    Full-index scoring dimensions

    Scores are category-relative percentile averages, then multiplied by the dimension weight.
    Minimum publication gate: all 7 dimensions represented and at least 15 of 25 measurable indicators plus source provenance. Each metric percentile requires at least 3 comparable banks in the same category. The investment score additionally requires P/B, P/E and 1-year/3-year total shareholder returns for at least 3 peers on the exact same market-data date. Unqualified rows receive no overall score or rank.
